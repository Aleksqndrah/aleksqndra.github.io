Nutrition Facts

2023

The survivor's kit for the cataclysm of our generation. During the pandemic, we were cut off from social gatherings, parties, and the time-wasters - distractions, that helped us push our problems aside. It turned out that we could still find all of this in the comfort of our own apartments, more specifically in our smartphones, which satisfied our dopamine cravings, or in a box of benzodiazepines, which provided the same sense of calm as a walk in the woods. Social media and the news are simply a stream of information whose composition we don’t know. The only thing that matters is that our need to “know” is satisfied. We don’t know what we’re consuming, what we’re feeding ourselves as we swallow one pill after another instead of working through our traumas or consciously deciding what information we want to take in. The inspiration for the project’s form came from military ration packs intended for consumption in harsh conditions. The size is similar to that of a modern smartphone.
